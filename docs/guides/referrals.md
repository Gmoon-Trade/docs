# Referrals

Bring creators to Gmoon and earn fees from the coins they launch.

The protocol takes 25% of every trading fee. A quarter of that part goes to the referrer of the coin's creator, which is 6.25% of each fee on the coin. See [Trading fees](../concepts/trading-fees.md).

For example, a 2% fee coin with $1M volume pays $20K in fees. The protocol part is $5K, and the referrer receives $1,250 from it.

## Create a referral link

Open your profile on [gmoon.trade](https://gmoon.trade) and register a referral code. A code has 1 to 32 characters and can use lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`. Codes are first come first served. Copy your link, which looks like `gmoon.trade/r/yourcode`, and share it.

## Join via a referral link

A user who opens a referral link is bound to that referrer. This happens once and can't be changed. Every coin that user launches afterwards is attributed to the referrer. Coins launched before binding are not.

## Claim referral rewards

You can claim referral rewards at any time in your profile. They are paid in USDG and are not converted into other assets like vault rewards.
