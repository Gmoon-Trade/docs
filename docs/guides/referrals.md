# Referrals

Bring creators to Gmoon and earn from the fees of the coins they launch.

## Get a code

Open your **Profile** on [gmoon.trade](https://gmoon.trade) and register a referral code. A code is 1 to 32 characters: lowercase letters, digits, `_` and `-`. Codes are first come first served. Copy your link, which looks like `gmoon.trade/r/yourcode`, and share it.

## Join with a code

A user who opens a referral link is bound to that referrer. This happens once and can't be changed. Every coin that user launches afterwards is attributed to the referrer. Coins launched before binding are not.

## What the referrer earns

The protocol takes 25% of every trading fee. 25% of that part goes to the referrer of the coin's creator, which is 6.25% of each fee on the coin. See [Trading fees](../concepts/trading-fees.md).

Example: a 2% fee coin with $1M volume pays $20K in fees. The protocol part is $5K, and the referrer receives $1,250.

## Claim

Open your **Profile** and claim. Rewards are paid in USDG.
