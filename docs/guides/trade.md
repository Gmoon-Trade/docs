# Trade

Open any coin on [gmoon.trade](https://gmoon.trade) and use the trade widget on its page. Every coin trades in its own Uniswap v4 pool against USDG, so trading is open from the first block. You can also trade the coins from any other trading terminal that supports Uniswap v4 pools on Robinhood Chain.

The first buy and the first sell of a coin ask for an approval. With a wallet that supports atomic batches (EIP-5792), the approval and the trade are sent as one confirmation.

## Buy a coin

1. Choose Buy.
2. Enter the amount of USDG. The widget shows how many coins you get and the minimum you accept after slippage (1% by default).
3. Confirm in your wallet.

## Sell a coin

1. Choose Sell.
2. Enter the amount of coins. The widget shows how much USDG you get and the minimum you accept after slippage (1% by default).
3. Confirm in your wallet.
