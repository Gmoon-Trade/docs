# Launch a coin

Launching a coin takes one transaction and a few minutes of setup. Open [gmoon.trade](https://gmoon.trade), connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain and start the coin creation. You pay only the network fee in ETH.

## 1. Coin details

- **Image**, uploaded from your device. It is required.
- **Name** and **Ticker**. Both are required.
- **Description**, and optionally your X account and website.

## 2. Reward assets

Pick from 1 to 8 assets that the coin's rewards are paid in: tokenized stocks, ETFs and crypto. Give each a weight. The weights add up to 100%. See [Reward assets](../concepts/reward-assets.md) for the list.

## 3. Fee and your share

- **Trading fee**: from 1% to 5% on every buy and sell of the coin. See [Trading fees](../concepts/trading-fees.md).
- **Creator share**: from 0% to 50% of the vault part of the fee. Depositors share the rest.

!!! warning "These can't be changed later"
    The trading fee, the creator share and the reward assets can't be changed after launch.

## 4. Optional first buy

You can buy part of your own coin in the launch transaction. Nobody can buy before you, and the buy pays the coin's own fee, not the [anti-sniper fee](../concepts/anti-sniper-fee.md). You can buy up to 80% of the supply. This is paid in USDG, so the wallet asks for an approval first.

## 5. Review and launch

Check the summary and the network fee, then confirm in your wallet. When the transaction is mined, the coin is live: its pool exists, trading is open from the first block and its Earn Vault is ready.

The coin starts at a market cap of about $20K with a supply of 1B coins, all in the pool.

## After the launch

- Claim your share of the rewards from the coin's page whenever you like. See [Earn](earn.md).
- Bring creators with your referral code and earn from their coins. See [Referrals](referrals.md).
- Launching from code? See [Launching a coin](../integration/launching-a-coin.md).
