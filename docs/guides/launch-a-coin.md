# Launch a coin

Launching a coin takes one transaction and a few minutes of setup. Open [gmoon.trade](https://gmoon.trade), connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain and start the coin creation. You pay only the network fee in ETH.

## Enter coin details

- Image, uploaded from your device. Required.
- Name. Required.
- Ticker. Required.
- Description. Optional.
- X account. Optional.
- Website. Optional.

## Choose reward assets

Pick 1 to 8 [supported assets](../concepts/reward-assets.md#supported-assets) to pay the coin's rewards in. Give each asset a weight from 0.01% to 100%. All weights together must be 100%.

## Set up fees

- Trading fee, from 1% to 5% on every buy and sell of the coin. See [Trading fees](../concepts/trading-fees.md).
- Creator share, from 0% to 50% of the vault part of the fee. Depositors share the rest.

!!! warning "These can't be changed later"
    The trading fee, the creator share and the reward assets can't be changed after launch.

## Make the first buy

You can buy part of your own coin in the launch transaction. Nobody can buy before you, and the buy pays the coin's own fee, not the [anti-sniper fee](../concepts/trading-fees.md#anti-sniper-fee). You can buy up to 80% of the supply. This is paid in USDG, so the wallet asks for an approval first.

## Review and launch

Check the summary and the network fee, then confirm in your wallet. When the transaction is mined, the coin is live. Its pool exists, trading is open from the first block and its Earn Vault is ready.

The coin has a supply of 1B coins, all in the pool. Its starting market cap is about $20K, before your first buy.

## After the launch

- Claim your creator rewards from the coin's page or from your profile.
- Deposit your coin in its Earn Vault to earn depositor rewards on top of your creator share. See [Earn](earn.md).
